# Meta-Atom

Meta-atoms are the subwavelength building blocks engineered to control the local properties of light within a metalens.

Instead of relying on the curved surfaces of conventional refractive optics, metalenses use arrays of these tiny nanostructures. Each meta-atom acts as an optical transformer, modifying the phase, amplitude, polarization, or direction of the incident electromagnetic field [1]. 

By altering geometric parameters like diameter, height, or shape, designers can tune the phase response of the transmitted wave. This allows flat, nanoscale surfaces to duplicate or surpass the functions of bulky glass lenses [2].

* Key parameters:
  * Subwavelength dimensions preventing diffraction.
  * Geometric parameters (width, height, period).
  * High-index dielectric or metallic materials.

> Takeaway: Meta-atoms replace bulk glass curvature with subwavelength geometric phase control.
